## SECTION 10 — PHASE 07: FEDERATE (network phase)

*Run on a schedule (monthly is the default) once two or more operators have submitted complete runs. One operator's tournament converges to a BLUEPRINT FINAL. Many operators' tournaments must converge to one global blueprint — or recruitment fragments the work instead of compounding it.*

**Submission.** After your run completes (Phases 01–06), submit to the canonical repository: a folder containing every phase output, your ledger, and a RUN-CARD (which models you used for which phases, total cost, anything unusual). Nothing cherry-picked. Partial runs are accepted but labeled partial — they score no records.

**The federation round.** On schedule, a synthesis agent (run by the steward or a delegated operator) executes:

1. **Collect** all submissions since the last round.
2. **Score** every run on the 17 dimensions (same ledger, same brutality).
3. **Global dimension records.** The global ledger holds the all-time best per dimension across every operator, every run, ever. A new record must be merged into the global blueprint or explicitly rejected — same burden of proof as Phase 06.
4. **Synthesize BLUEPRINT v(K+1)** from BLUEPRINT vK + all new submissions, under the identical merge-or-reject rules: only what improves, never dilution, one-sentence justifications, covenant supremacy.
5. **Publish** the new global blueprint + changelog (every substantive change listed) + updated records table to the canonical repository.

**Rules.** The covenant binds federation too — a submission that violates it is rejected with the reason stated, no matter its scores. Contaminated blind phases disqualify the run's reckoning and mind scores. Model identities must be disclosed per phase — a run that hides which model did what is rejected. Any operator may fork the blueprint; forks are legitimate but don't move global records unless re-merged. The steward arbitrates disputes but cannot change these rules unilaterally.
