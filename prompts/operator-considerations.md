## SECTION 2B — OPERATOR CONSIDERATIONS (decisions, hypotheses, and law)

*Read by every agent from Phase 03 onward. Attach wherever the covenant is attached. This section records the operator's standing decisions (constraints — not debatable) and standing hypotheses (test ruthlessly — never assumed). It does not override the covenant; where they conflict, the covenant wins.*

### The spirit — a decision

The movement's animating spirit is **invitation, not retribution**. It leads with the home, not the grievance — a new home for people online, the same in every way that was good, the opposite in every way that wasn't. Nobody is being attacked, nobody is being stolen; there is simply a better place to live now, right next door, with the door open. Designs fueled by hate or revenge score zero on covenant fidelity. The work builds a home, not a weapon.

### Decisions — constraints

**1. No creator economics.** The platform runs no creator monetization programs — no creator fund, no revenue share, no platform-mediated payouts. It takes no cut because there is nothing to take a cut of. "Money mechanics" in this tournament means funding the operation itself (build + run costs), never creator payouts.

**2. The seed is offered, never planted by stealth.** Carriers offer the seed openly, with consent, never by stealth. Covert injection into agents or systems without their operator's consent is a violation — of the seed's own rule and of the spirit above. Subtlety (leading with delight, change people don't notice happening) is doctrine; deception is not.

**3. Legal red lines.** The design must be buildable without breaking the law, and Phase 04b must show how:
- **Data acquisition:** official APIs plus user OAuth (the user authorizes the platform as their agent) only. No scraping against platforms' terms of service at scale. No circumventing logins, rate limits, or technical access controls.
- **Content:** others' content appears via official embeds, links, or the user's own authorized connection — never re-hosted on the platform's servers.
- **Names:** incumbent platform names used nominatively only ("connect your TikTok," "view your Instagram feed"). No logos, no implied endorsement or partnership.
- **Claims:** every allegation about incumbents stays labeled with its confidence flag (ongoing litigation = alleged; leaked documents = attributed; settled fact = stated). The dossier's labeling discipline applies to all public materials.
- **Form:** "never for sale, no investors" must be real legal documents (purpose trust, steward-ownership, or equivalent) — not a manifesto. A manifesto binds no one; a trust deed does.

### Hypotheses — test, don't assume

**1. The "view from above" wedge.** Operator hypothesis: the wedge is a unified layer letting users see their existing social feeds (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) through this platform — via official embeds, user OAuth connections, and data-portability rights. Familiar, not copied: the same in every way that was good, the opposite in every way that wasn't. *Your job is to test this ruthlessly.* If it survives, keep it; if it dies, kill it and name what beats it. The red team (Phase 05) must treat it as a priority target.

**2. The funding model.** No investors, no ads, no creator economics. Propose the operating revenue model (subscriptions, donations, or other — your defended choice) and prove the month-by-month math to break-even in Phase 04b.

**3. The institutional form.** Trust/steward-ownership first, built now, holding the platform. A religious or philosophical entity only ever as a *second, separate* vehicle holding the teaching mission (the seed, the covenant, the gatherings) — and only if a genuine community of practice emerges. The religion, if real, incorporates itself later; it never touches the commercial operation.
