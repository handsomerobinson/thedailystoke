# The beacon — captured, not started

Notes only. Nothing here is designed or scheduled yet.

---

## The idea

A small voice-first device. No screen. You talk to it, it talks back. It is
the interface to the cocoon when your hands and eyes are busy — driving,
walking, working.

Not a phone. Not a phone accessory. A beacon.

## Where it came from

Driving, dictating instead of holding the phone. Eyes up. And the observation
that mattered:

> "I see so much more in the world now, because I'm not looking at my maps.
> I can move freely about the cabin, I see little things, little intricate
> details about the world — because when we weren't looking at our phones all
> the time, we were seeing what was around us. I saw this cute little barn
> that I'm going to go back and look at, because it's cute. Everything's so
> corporatized, that it's a challenge — but there are still these beautiful
> little gems, and if you're moving slow enough to see them, that is life
> changing."

The product is not "voice control". The product is **getting your eyes back**.
The thing you gain is what you notice.

## Why it belongs to this project

It is the third piece, and the arc only works in order:

```
vault   -> the stuff exists somewhere you own
cocoon  -> the stuff is searchable and knows you
beacon  -> you reach it without looking down
```

The beacon is useless without a cocoon behind it — a voice assistant with
nothing personal to draw on is just a worse Siri. But once the cocoon exists,
the beacon is the natural way to use it, and the reason the phone can finally
stay in a pocket.

Capture something on the beacon and it lands in the vault. That loop is
already most of the way built.

## Open questions for later

- **The brain has to be much better.** That is the note. Current voice
  assistants fail because they are shallow, not because they are voice.
  The cocoon is what could make it deep.
- Dedicated hardware, or does this start as an iOS app that just never asks
  you to look at it?
- What does it do with no signal? The cocoon lives on a tailnet at home.
- Capture-only first, or conversational from the start?
- "Remind me about that barn" — location plus a voice note plus a thing seen.
  Probably the smallest real demo of the whole idea.
